## Icons at render time

`mermaid-studio` and `mermaid-architecture-iconify` can fetch Iconify icon packs when a
diagram is rendered. Each pack has its own licence (for example `logos` and `simple-icons`
are CC0, `mdi` is Apache-2.0, Font Awesome `fa6-*` is CC BY 4.0 and needs attribution in
what you publish). Nothing from these packs ships in this plugin.
